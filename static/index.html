How it works
  1. Open a board on Pinterest and tap Share → Copy link.
  2. Paste it above and press Find pins.
  3. Check the preview, then hit Download all.
Works with public boards. Images are streamed straight from Pinterest into your ZIP. Nothing is stored on this server. Only download boards you own or have permission to use. Not affiliated with Pinterest.
